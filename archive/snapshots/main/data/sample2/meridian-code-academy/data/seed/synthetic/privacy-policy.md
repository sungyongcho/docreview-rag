# Meridian Code Academy — Privacy & Data Retention Policy

Meridian Code Academy ("MCA") is a fictional peer-learning coding school used as sample
content for a document-review demo. No real persons, policies, or data are described.

## 1. Controller and Contact

MCA determines the purposes and means of processing student personal data. Questions and
rights requests go to the Data Protection Officer at privacy@meridiancode.example.

## 2. Data We Collect

Depending on a student's status, MCA may collect: email, name, date of birth, postal
address, photograph, emergency contact, admissions-test results, support tickets, and
technical data such as IP address and browser collected automatically when browsing the
intranet.

## 3. Retention Periods

### 3.1 Account and authentication data
Retained for 24 months after the last activity on the intranet, unless an earlier deletion
request is made.

### 3.2 Connection logs
Retained for 6 months for security monitoring.

### 3.3 Backups
Retained for 90 days.

### 3.4 Video-surveillance footage
Retained for 21 days, except footage preserved for an open security incident.

### 3.5 Access-control badge events
Retained for 3 months.

### 3.6 Wi-Fi connection data
Retained for 12 months.

### 3.7 Failed admissions results
Deleted 14 days after a candidate fails the selection.

### 3.8 Academic transcripts and school records
Archived for 40 years after the student leaves MCA.

### 3.9 Apprenticeship contracts
Retained for 6 years after the contract end date.

### 3.10 Marketing contact data
Retained for 18 months after the contact's last engagement.

### 3.11 Alumni community profile
Retained until the alumnus requests deletion.

### 3.12 Whistleblowing reports with no action taken
Deleted 2 months after the verification is closed.

## 4. Legal Bases

Processing relies, depending on the purpose, on consent, the performance of a contract,
compliance with a legal obligation, or MCA's legitimate interest in operating and securing
its platforms.

## 5. Your Rights

Students may access, rectify, erase, or port their data, and may object to or restrict
processing. MCA responds within 1 month, extendable once for complex requests.

## 6. Cookies

### 6.1 Audience cookies
Stored for at most 13 months.

### 6.2 Data collected via cookies
Retained for at most 25 months.

## 7. International Transfers

Data is stored primarily within the European Union. Where data is transferred outside the
EU, MCA relies on Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard.
