# Meridian Code Academy — Enrollment & Progression

This document describes how candidates are admitted and how students progress. Sample
content for a document-review demo.

## 1. Admissions

### 1.1 Stages
Admission begins with an online pre-admission quiz (results within 48 hours), followed by an
ID check and a selection bootcamp.

### 1.2 Selection bootcamp
The selection bootcamp is an intensive test held over 21 days. Results are published within
10 days of the end of the bootcamp.

## 2. Ranks

### 2.1 Trainee
A candidate taking the selection bootcamp.

### 2.2 Cadet
A student who passed the bootcamp and enrolled in the Core curriculum.

### 2.3 Graduate
A student who completed the Core capstone project.

### 2.4 Alumnus
A student who completed the Core and joined the alumni network.

## 3. Alumni Transition

### 3.1 Manual transition
A Graduate may activate alumni status at any time after completing the Core. This action is
irreversible.

### 3.2 Automatic transition
Alumni transition occurs automatically after 6 months of inactivity following completion of
the Core, regardless of level reached.

## 4. Progression System

### 4.1 Deadline system
A rolling deadline (the "wall") sets a minimum pace. Failing the wall permanently ends the
curriculum.

### 4.2 Experience
Completing projects earns experience that postpones the wall.

### 4.3 Freeze
A student may suspend studies with a freeze. During a freeze, intranet and premises access
are suspended and the wall is postponed. The total freeze duration is capped at 6 months.

## 5. Account Inactivity

After 2 years of inactivity, or following a disciplinary decision, a student's intranet
access may be closed.
