# Meridian Code Academy — Examination Rules

These rules apply to all exams held in the exam clusters. Sample content for a
document-review demo. Breaching any rule may lead to exclusion from the exam and further
sanctions.

## 1. Before the Exam

Pockets must be empty. Each student presents their student card on entry; without a card,
access is refused. Once the exam has started, latecomers are not admitted.

## 2. Prohibited Items

Phones, watches, and other connected devices must be switched off and stored in the
student's bag. Personal peripherals such as keyboards and mice may not be used.

## 3. During the Exam

### 3.1 Materials
Only blank loose-leaf paper may be used for drafts; notebooks are forbidden. A pen or pencil
and a water bottle placed on the floor are allowed. Draft sheets are returned at the end.

### 3.2 Movement
Leaving the seat is forbidden except to quit the exam or to use the toilet. Staff offer a
toilet break every 45 minutes.

### 3.3 Communication
Any attempt to communicate with another student, by any means, is treated as cheating and
may result in exclusion.

## 4. Exam Length

Exams last between 2 and 6 hours depending on the exam. The duration of each exam is shown on
the intranet.

## 5. Cheating

The following are cheating: looking at a phone or any connected device; exchanging
information with another student; bringing draft sheets with pre-written code; or hiding
solutions on or off the workstation. Cheating is a Tier 3 offense and is referred to the
Disciplinary Board, which may impose temporary or permanent exclusion.
