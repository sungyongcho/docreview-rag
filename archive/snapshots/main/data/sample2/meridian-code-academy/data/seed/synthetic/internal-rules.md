# Meridian Code Academy — Campus Internal Rules

These Rules govern conduct at the MCA campus. Sample content for a document-review demo.

## 1. Scope and Acceptance

These Rules apply to all students from the start of their studies and are accepted on first
login to the intranet. Where documents conflict, the following order applies, higher first:
(1) these Internal Rules, (2) the premises-access rules, (3) the IT & Security Charter. The
most recently signed version of a document prevails over earlier versions.

## 2. Modification

Management may revise these Rules. Changes are communicated to students by email and are
binding once notified.

## 3. Campus Access

### 3.1 Hours
Registered students have access to the campus 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, year-round,
except where access is suspended for disciplinary reasons.

### 3.2 Badge
Each student is issued a personal RFID badge. The badge is strictly personal and may not be
lent or transferred.

### 3.3 Badge replacement
A lost badge is replaced at a cost of EUR 8 for the first replacement and EUR 15 for each
subsequent replacement.

## 4. Visitors

### 4.1 Invitation
Visitors may enter only when invited by a registered student. Candidates may not bring
visitors.

### 4.2 Limits
A visit may not exceed 2 hours. The visitor presents ID at reception, which is held for the
duration of the visit.

## 5. Facilities

### 5.1 Lockers
Lockers are for temporary use only and may not be used for more than 72 hours. Storing food
in lockers is forbidden. Students must reset the locker code after each use.

### 5.2 Gym
The gym is reserved for students and staff; guests are not permitted. Users must bring a
towel and clean the equipment after use.

## 6. Conduct and Messaging

Communications on MCA tools must remain courteous and lawful. The following are prohibited:
illegal content; harassing, discriminatory, or hateful messages; spam; advertising outside
dedicated channels; and sharing another person's personal data without consent.

## 7. Disciplinary Sanctions

Breaches are sanctioned by community-service ("CS") hours or referral to the Disciplinary
Board, depending on severity.

### 7.1 Tier 1 — minimum 3 hours of community service
Examples: holding food or a non-water drink at a cluster workstation; failing to tidy a
workstation after use; entering or leaving the building without badging; losing a student
card on campus.

### 7.2 Tier 2 — minimum 6 hours of community service
Examples: smoking or vaping in a prohibited area; leaving an invited visitor unattended;
refusing to follow staff instructions; bringing an animal onto campus without authorization.

### 7.3 Tier 3 — referral to the Disciplinary Board, up to permanent exclusion
Examples: bringing or consuming alcohol outside an authorized event; harassment or
discriminatory or hateful messages; theft; impersonating another person; running a security
test against a target without authorization; cheating in the curriculum or in an exam.

### 7.4 Repeat offenses
A third failure to complete assigned community service is referred to the Disciplinary Board.

## 8. Disciplinary Board Procedure

The Board has 3 to 5 members. A student is notified of the charges at least 5 working days
before the hearing, may be assisted by one person of their choice, and receives the decision
in writing with reasons and appeal information.

## 9. Intellectual Property

The MCA name, logos, and all course and exam materials are MCA property. Reproducing or
redistributing them without prior written consent is prohibited and is a disciplinary
offense.
