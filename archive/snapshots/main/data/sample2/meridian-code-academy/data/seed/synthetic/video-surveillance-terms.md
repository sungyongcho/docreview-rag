# Meridian Code Academy — Terms of Use for Video Surveillance

How MCA operates video surveillance on its premises. Sample content for a document-review
demo.

## 1. Purpose

Video surveillance protects the safety of people and property and acts as a deterrent.

## 2. Coverage

Cameras cover only entrances and circulation areas. They are never positioned to view
individual workstations or living areas.

## 3. Operation

Cameras operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, year-round.

## 4. Retention

Footage is retained for 21 days. Footage related to an open security incident is preserved
on separate storage until the incident procedure is closed. This matches the Privacy & Data
Retention Policy.

## 5. Access

Only authorized security staff, and the police in the event of an incident, may view the
footage. Maintenance staff of the camera-system provider may access it solely for
maintenance. Access is protected by appropriate security measures.

## 6. Legal Basis

Processing relies on MCA's legitimate interest in ensuring the safety of people and property.

## 7. Rights

Individuals may request access to or deletion of footage concerning them, and may object to
or restrict the processing, by contacting the Data Protection Officer at
privacy@meridiancode.example.

## 8. Information of Data Subjects

Beyond these terms, individuals are informed about the video surveillance through signage
posted at entrances and in rooms, and through the Internal Rules.
