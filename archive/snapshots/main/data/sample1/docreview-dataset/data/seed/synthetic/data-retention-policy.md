# Northwind Labs — Data Retention Policy

This policy defines how long Northwind Labs retains different categories of data and how
deletion is handled. Sample content for a document-review demo.

## 1. Scope

This policy covers all customer, operational, and business records held by Northwind Labs.

## 2. Retention Periods

### 2.1 Customer account records
Customer account records are retained for 7 years after account closure.

### 2.2 Application logs
Application and system logs are retained for 90 days.

### 2.3 Marketing contact data
Marketing contact data is retained for 24 months after the contact's last engagement.

### 2.4 Financial records
Financial records are retained for 7 years to meet accounting and audit obligations.

## 3. Deletion

### 3.1 Routine deletion
Data that has passed its retention period must be deleted within 30 days.

### 3.2 Customer deletion requests
Verified customer deletion requests must be fulfilled within 30 days of identity
verification.

## 4. Ownership

### 4.1 Review cadence
The Data Protection Officer owns the retention schedule and reviews it annually.
