# Northwind Labs — Security & Acceptable Use Policy

This policy defines baseline security requirements for all Northwind Labs systems and data.
Sample content for a document-review demo.

## 1. Authentication

### 1.1 Passwords
Account passwords must be at least 14 characters long.

### 1.2 Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company systems. Access without MFA
is prohibited.

## 2. Devices

### 2.1 Encryption
All company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled before any company data is stored
on them.

### 2.2 Auto-lock
Devices must be configured to automatically lock after 10 minutes of inactivity.

## 3. Data Classification

### 3.1 Levels
All data is classified as one of four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted.

### 3.2 Handling Restricted data
Restricted data must not be stored on personal devices under any circumstances.

## 4. AI Tool Usage

### 4.1 Approved use
Employees may use approved AI assistants when working with Public and Internal data only.

### 4.2 Prohibited use
Confidential or Restricted data must not be pasted into third-party AI tools.

## 5. Incident Reporting

### 5.1 Timeline
Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team within 1 hour of
discovery.

### 5.2 Lost devices
Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately and will be remotely wiped.
