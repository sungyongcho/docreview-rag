# Northwind Labs — Vendor & Procurement Policy

This policy defines the approvals and reviews required before engaging a vendor. Sample
content for a document-review demo. Contract values below are annualized.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all new vendor engagements and renewals.

## 2. Approval Thresholds

### 2.1 Manager approval
New vendor contracts under $10,000 per year require manager approval.

### 2.2 Security review threshold
Contracts of $10,000 per year or more require a completed security review before signature.

### 2.3 VP and Legal approval
Contracts of $50,000 per year or more additionally require VP and Legal approval.

## 3. Security Review

### 3.1 Data-based trigger
Vendors that will handle Confidential or Restricted data must complete a security review
regardless of contract value.

### 3.2 Renewal
A vendor security review must be renewed every 12 months to remain valid.

## 4. Contract Terms

### 4.1 Data processing addendum
All contracts must include a data processing addendum (DPA) when personal data will be
shared with the vendor.

### 4.2 Payment terms
Payment terms default to net-30 unless other terms are negotiated and documented.
