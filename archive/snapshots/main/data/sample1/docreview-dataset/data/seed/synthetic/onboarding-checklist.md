# Northwind Labs — New-Hire Onboarding Checklist

A structured checklist for onboarding new employees. Sample content for a document-review
demo. Items marked "required" are mandatory completion gates.

## 1. Before Day 1

### 1.1 Account provisioning
IT provisions email and core system accounts before the start date.

### 1.2 Hardware
A company laptop is shipped to arrive on or before the start date.

## 2. Day 1 (required)

### 2.1 Identity and policy
The new hire completes identity verification and signs the Security & Acceptable Use Policy.

### 2.2 MFA
The new hire enables multi-factor authentication on all assigned accounts on Day 1.

## 3. Week 1 (required)

### 3.1 Security training
The new hire completes mandatory security-awareness training within the first week.

### 3.2 Onboarding buddy
The new hire meets their assigned onboarding buddy.

## 4. Day 30

### 4.1 Role training
The new hire completes role-specific training modules by Day 30.

## 5. Day 90

### 5.1 Probation review
The manager completes the probation review with the new hire at Day 90.

### 5.2 Remote-work status
Remote-work eligibility is confirmed at the probation review, consistent with the Employee
Handbook remote-work eligibility rule.
