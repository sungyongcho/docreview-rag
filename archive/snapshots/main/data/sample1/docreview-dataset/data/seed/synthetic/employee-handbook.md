# Northwind Labs — Employee Handbook

Northwind Labs is a fictional ~400-person B2B SaaS company. This handbook is sample
content created for a document-review demo and does not describe any real organization.

## 1. Working Hours

The standard workweek is 40 hours. Core collaboration hours are 10:00–16:00 in the
employee's local time zone; employees may schedule the remaining hours flexibly with
manager agreement.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO)

### 2.1 New-hire accrual
New employees accrue PTO at a rate of 1.5 days per month, for a total of 18 days per
calendar year.

### 2.2 Tenured accrual
Employees with three or more years of continuous service accrue 22 days per calendar year.

### 2.3 Carryover
Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried into the next calendar year. Any unused days beyond
5 are forfeited on December 31.

## 3. Sick Leave

### 3.1 Allowance
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year. Sick leave is separate from PTO and
does not roll over.

### 3.2 Documentation
A medical note is required for any sick absence longer than 3 consecutive working days.

## 4. Remote Work

### 4.1 Eligibility
Employees become eligible to work remotely after completing the 90-day probation period
described in Section 5.

### 4.2 Limits
Remote work is capped at 3 days per week unless the role is formally designated as fully
remote in the employee's offer letter.

## 5. Probation

### 5.1 Duration
All new employees serve a 90-day probation period beginning on their start date.

### 5.2 Termination during probation
During probation, either the employee or Northwind Labs may end the employment relationship
with 1 week of written notice.

## 6. Parental Leave

### 6.1 Allowance
Eligible employees receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Eligibility begins after
12 months of continuous employment.
