# Northwind Labs — Expense & Reimbursement Policy

This policy defines reimbursable business expenses and the approvals required. Sample
content for a document-review demo.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all employees and contractors who incur business expenses on behalf
of Northwind Labs.

## 2. Approval Thresholds

### 2.1 Manager approval
Expenses up to and including $500 require manager approval.

### 2.2 Director approval
Expenses from $500.01 to $5,000 require director approval.

### 2.3 VP approval
Expenses above $5,000 require VP approval and a Finance review before reimbursement.

## 3. Travel

### 3.1 Short flights
Economy class is required for all flights shorter than 6 hours.

### 3.2 Long flights
Premium economy is permitted for flights of 6 hours or longer.

### 3.3 Business class
Business class requires VP approval regardless of flight length.

## 4. Meals

### 4.1 Travel per diem
The daily meal per diem while traveling is $75.

### 4.2 Client meals
Client meals are reimbursable up to $150 per attendee. Within that total, alcohol is capped
at $50 per attendee.

### 4.3 Local meals
Meals during local, non-travel workdays are not reimbursable.

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Items

### 5.1 Excluded items
Personal entertainment, fines and penalties, and personal travel upgrades are not
reimbursable.

## 6. Submission

### 6.1 Deadline
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.

### 6.2 Receipts
An itemized receipt is required for any single expense of $25 or more.
